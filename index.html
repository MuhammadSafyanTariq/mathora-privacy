01

Introduction

Mathora ("the App") is developed and published by RapidRise Tech ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why, and what happens to it. It applies to the Mathora mobile app on Android and iOS.

02

Scope

This policy covers the Mathora app itself. It does not cover the app stores you downloaded it from (Google Play, the App Store), which have their own privacy practices.

04

Information you provide

None. There is no sign-up form, profile, comment box, or upload feature anywhere in the app. You cannot type anything into Mathora that leaves your device.

05

Automatically collected information

Mathora contains no analytics library, no crash-reporting library, and no device-fingerprinting code of any kind. We do not automatically receive usage statistics, session logs, or diagnostic reports from your device. Standard information the app stores themselves generate (install counts, aggregate ratings, crash rates from the OS's own dashboards) is governed by Google's and Apple's own developer-facing policies, not by Mathora's code.

06

On-device storage

Mathora stores two things locally, entirely within your device's app storage:

  • A content database — the questions, exercises, and solutions for classes 9–12, copied into a local SQLite database the first time you open the app, so every screen after that works offline.
  • One preference flag — whether you've completed the onboarding walkthrough, so it doesn't show again.

Neither of these is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device settings, deletes both completely.

07

Third-party services

Mathora's typography is rendered using Google Fonts (via the open-source google_fonts Flutter package). Depending on your device and platform, the app may need to download the font files it uses (Poppins) the first time they're needed, which involves a request to Google's font-delivery servers and exposes your device's IP address to Google in the same way any web request would, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. No other data is sent as part of this request, and it is not used by us for tracking or advertising.

We do not use Firebase, any backend server, any analytics SDK, any advertising SDK, or any authentication provider. If that changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

08

Advertising, analytics & tracking

Mathora shows no ads, contains no ad SDK, and does not use your data — or anyone's — for advertising, personalization, or cross-app tracking. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is being collected for these purposes in the first place.

09

Accounts & sign-in

Not applicable. Mathora does not offer account creation, sign-in, or any form of authentication. Every user has the same experience without identifying themselves in any way.

10

Payments & subscriptions

Not applicable. Mathora has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid tiers, and processes no payment information.

11

Push notifications

Not applicable. Mathora does not request notification permission and has no notification functionality.

12

Educational content & usage

The app does not track which class, chapter, exercise, or question you view. There is no learning-progress, streak, or performance record kept anywhere — on your device or off it.

13

How we use information

The two items stored locally (§6) are used only to run the app itself: showing content offline, and remembering that you've seen the onboarding screens. We have no server to send anything to, and nothing described in this policy is used for profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond making the app work.

14

How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. The one qualified exception is the incidental font-delivery request to Google described in §7, which is a standard technical dependency, not a data-sharing arrangement we control the content of.

15

Data retention

Everything Mathora stores lives on your device and stays there for as long as the app is installed. There is no server-side copy to retain or expire, because there is no server. Retention is entirely in your hands: uninstall the app, or clear its storage, and it's gone.

16

Data security

Because Mathora doesn't transmit personal data to us, the primary security surface is your own device's built-in app sandboxing — the same protection every app on Android and iOS relies on. We follow standard release practices but cannot make guarantees about the security of your device itself.

17

Your rights & data deletion

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to look up, export, or delete on request — a deletion or access request to us would return no records. If you'd like confirmation of that in writing, or have any concern about this, contact us using the details in §22.

18

Children's privacy

Mathora is built for students in grades 9–12 — an age range that includes minors under Pakistani and most other education systems. Because the app collects no personal information from any user, of any age, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and there is no mechanism in the app through which a child (or anyone) could submit personal information even if they wanted to.

19

International data transfers

Not applicable in the conventional sense — since no personal data is transmitted to us, there is no cross-border transfer of your data by us to track. The only outbound request the app can make is the font-delivery request in §7, to Google's own global infrastructure.

20

Third-party links

The app itself contains no outbound links to other websites or apps. This policy and any store listing pages may link elsewhere (e.g. to this Privacy Policy from Google Play); those destinations have their own privacy practices.

21

Changes to this policy

If Mathora's functionality changes in a way that affects what this policy says — for example, if we ever add accounts, analytics, or ads — we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version reaches the store.

22
