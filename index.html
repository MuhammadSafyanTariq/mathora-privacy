Privacy Policy for Mathora

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Mathora ("the app", "we", "us") is a study/math learning app for Android. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Data Collection

Mathora does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or device data with us or any third party. The app has no network access and does not connect to the internet.

Data Stored on Your Device

Mathora stores some information locally on your device only, to make the app work (for example, your progress, saved preferences, and app settings). This data is stored using local device storage and a local database, stays on your device, and is never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Permissions

Mathora does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive device permissions.

Third-Party Services

Mathora does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Mathora does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: hello@rapidrisetech.com
